Problem space

Neurosurgical outcomes are still heavily shaped by late intervention.

Neurosurgical care often involves complex decisions made across symptoms, care history, follow-up visits, and clinical observations. AgiNeuro is exploring how future neurosurgical technologies can support earlier understanding, better decision-making, and more responsive care.

01

Clinical uncertainty

Neurological changes can be difficult to assess before a problem becomes clinically obvious.

02

Fragmented clinical signals

Patient symptoms, procedural context, clinician notes, and longitudinal follow-up often sit in separate workflows.

03

Personalized intervention

Neurosurgical care is moving toward more adaptive, patient-specific tools, but the clinical path remains complex.

Our approach

Broad enough to learn, focused enough to matter.

AgiNeuro is intentionally early and clinician-informed. Rather than publicly narrowing around one exact mechanism, the team is validating opportunities across neurosurgical care, brain-focused intervention, and clinical workflow support.

  • Map unmet needs in neurosurgical monitoring, follow-up, and decision support.
  • Explore various methodologies to identify clinical risk earlier.
  • Prototype through staged validation, stakeholder feedback, simulation, and bench testing.

Technology direction

A staged technology direction grounded in clinical reality.

AgiNeuro is focused on building clinically useful technologies that support safer, clearer, and more responsive neurosurgical care. Our expertise spans engineering, clinical workflows, and brain-focused intervention.

Neuroengineering layer

  • Connect clinical, procedural, and patient-reported signals into a clearer picture.
  • Support earlier recognition of changes that may require attention.
  • Build toward tools that fit realistic hospital, specialist, and surgical workflows.

Clinical systems layer

  • Explore patient-specific response patterns.
  • Study feedback-driven concepts for safer and more responsive neurological care.
  • Validate gradually through simulation, benchtop systems, and VOC Feedback.

Roadmap

A careful path from problem discovery to prototype.

1

Clinical discovery

Interviewing clinicians, researchers, and stakeholders to identify urgent neurosurgical care pain points.

2

Use-case definition

Narrowing the first wedge based on feasibility, clinical value, regulatory path, and stakeholder pull.

3

Technical validation

Building early models and benchtop prototypes to test validate different mechanism pipelines.

4

Translation planning

Refining the direction around manufacturability/repeatability as well as potential for translation.
